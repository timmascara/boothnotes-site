The Keeper never said the boy’s name the way the mainland did. “Eoghan,” she said, the old way, and the ferrymen gave up correcting her. He had come across at eleven with one bag and a cough, and he stayed because nobody sent for him.

“You’ll keep this light one day,” the Keeper told him. Eoghan, who had never been promised anything, wrote it on the wall of the lamp room where she would never look.

What prep looks like with it.

Mark once, everywhere

Select a word, add how you say it, pick a color. Every occurrence in the book is highlighted, and the note travels with all of them.

Your voice, on the word

Record yourself saying it once. Tap the word anywhere in the book and hear it back. No more scrolling to the front for the cheat sheet.

Your cast

A character can have more than one name. “Maren” and “the Keeper” share one color and one voice memo, so however she is named, you hear how you decided she sounds.

Apple Pencil ink

Scribble in the margins with pressure-aware, low-latency ink. Your finger still scrolls. A pen tap on a word opens its note instead of drawing.

Built in a real booth

Prep on the iPad. Record with the same manuscript open.

Booth Notes was built by a working narrator for his own sessions. Prep on the couch with the Pencil, then open the same book on the Mac in the booth, every note and every memo already there. The Guide lists every word and every character, searchable, with page jumps, and it exports.

Questions narrators ask.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap “Not now” on the first screen and every feature works on that device. An account only exists to sync across devices, and that is what Pro pays for.

I work under NDA. Where does the manuscript go?

Nowhere, unless you turn sync on. Without sync the PDF, your notes and your memos never leave the device. With sync they are stored in your own account, readable only by you, and deleting the account deletes them.

What files does it take?

PDF manuscripts. If a publisher sends a Word file, export it to PDF first. Backups are a single file you can keep anywhere and restore on any device.

Does it work on the Mac?

Yes. It is the iPad app running on Apple Silicon Macs from the Mac App Store, with recording and playback. There is no Pencil on a Mac, so ink is read-only there.
