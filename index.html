Booth Notes

Manuscript prep for audiobook narrators.

You prep on the iPad. You record in the booth. Booth Notes is the same manuscript in both places — iPad, iPhone and Mac.

Mark a word once, and every occurrence in the book is highlighted. Three hundred pages later, when the name comes back and you can't remember what you decided, it's already glowing.

What it does

  • Mark once, everywhere. One pronunciation note covers the whole manuscript.
  • Voice memos. Record yourself saying it; play it back with one tap.
  • Apple Pencil ink. Pressure-aware margin notes — your finger still scrolls.
  • Character guide. Every marked word, searchable, with page jumps.
  • Series import. The next book already knows how to say everyone's name.
  • Offline first. Sign-in is optional. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Built in a real booth, for real sessions.
