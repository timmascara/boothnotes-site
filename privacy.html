Booth Notes

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Booth Notes is manuscript prep for audiobook narrators. You work under NDA on books nobody has read yet, so the short version matters: your manuscripts are yours. The app works completely offline, we don't run ads or analytics, and nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone.

You can use Booth Notes without an account. If you never sign in, nothing ever leaves your device — there is no server holding your work, because you never gave us one.

What stays on your device

Whether or not you sign in, Booth Notes stores everything you create in your device's own app storage:

  • The manuscript PDFs you import
  • The words you mark, with their pronunciations, notes and sample sentences
  • Your voice memos
  • Your Apple Pencil ink
  • Small settings, such as which page of a book you were last reading

Deleting the app from your device deletes all of it.

What happens if you turn on sync

Signing in is optional and exists for one reason: so the same library appears on your iPad, your iPhone and your Mac. When you are signed in, the items listed above are copied to our cloud storage, provided by Supabase, and we also hold:

  • Your email address, as the name of your account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address — not your real one.
  • Your first name, if Sign in with Apple passes one along. It is used only to greet you in the app.

Your cloud copy is private to your account. Access is enforced at the database and file-storage level, so one account cannot read another's manuscripts, notes or recordings.

We use this information for exactly one purpose: making sync work. We do not use it for advertising, we do not profile you, and we do not share or sell it.

Booth Notes Pro

Sync is sold as a subscription through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, your Apple ID or your purchase history. The app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device whether a subscription is active and keeps only that yes-or-no answer, on the device.

What we do not do

  • No tracking. There are no advertising identifiers, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no data brokers.
  • No analytics. Booth Notes contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We cannot see which features you use or which books you open.
  • No ads.
  • No selling or sharing your content. Not to publishers, not to AI training, not to anyone.

Your microphone

Booth Notes asks for microphone access so you can record a voice memo on a word — you saying the name the way you decided to say it. Recording only happens when you tap record. The audio is saved with that word, on your device, and copied to your cloud account only if you are signed in. It is never streamed anywhere, transcribed, or used to train anything.

Pronunciation lookup links

The word editor offers shortcuts to look a word up on Forvo, YouGlish and Merriam-Webster. Nothing is sent to those sites unless you tap one of those buttons, and when you do, the only thing sent is the single word you are looking up. Those sites are independent of Booth Notes and have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep things

Your cloud copy exists for as long as your account does. You can remove it at any time:

  • Delete one book and its notes, memos and ink are removed from the cloud and from your other signed-in devices.
  • Sign out and choose "Remove from this device" to clear a device while keeping your cloud library.
  • Delete your account — in the app, tap your account chip, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored in the cloud for it: manuscripts, notes, voice memos and ink. It cannot be undone. Anything already on a device stays on that device, where it is yours to keep or delete.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold. In practice the app already gives you all four: your data is visible in the app, editable in the app, exportable with the backup button, and deletable with the account-deletion button. If you would rather ask a person, write to us at the address below and we will do it.

Children

Booth Notes is a professional tool for narrators and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
